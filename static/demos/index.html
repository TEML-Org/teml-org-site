TEML v-alpha-003: a smaller language

Each tab shows one feature as a small model, then everything together in the hotel model. Every board is drawn directly from the TEML shown under it. Click any sticky or slice heading for its details and Given/When/Then specs.

What changed: references are plain names (no &, * or <<), view updates are - View: [props], every slice has an events list, wfes are now automations, and an automation always works from a to-do view. Compare the TEML under each board with v-alpha-002.
